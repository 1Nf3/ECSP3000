\section*{PERT \& Gantt Chart}
The PERT chart maps out the dependencies of the different tasks \& milestones within my project. The values on the graph use
an expected time - in days - calculated from the optimistic, most likely and pessimistic time estimates. \\  \\  
The stretch track will be developed in parallel with the rest of the project starting after initial research is complete. A decision will be made after milestone \textbf{M3} (Est. January) as to if a complete rootkit design will be pursed, contigent on time \& feasibility. \\ \\
The PERT Expected Time values were then used to produce a Gantt Chart, mapped against calendar months to help better visualise my project's planned progress against milestones \& deadlines.
